Privacy Policy

Ollie · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Ollie is a personal organization app for capturing thoughts, organizing work and goals, and building routines. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. Ollie is currently in beta testing.

Information we collect

  • Account information — the email address you use to sign up and authenticate.
  • Content you create — notes, tasks, goals, habits, finance entries, and other items you add to the app.
  • Push notification token — if you enable reminders, so we can deliver notifications to your device.
  • Diagnostic data — crash reports and app-performance information, including session diagnostics, used to find and fix bugs.

AI features

To sort a brain-dump note into the right parts of the app, Ollie sends it to its own AI service. This happens for every brain dump. Before any outside AI model sees the text, Ollie removes names, contact details, ID-like numbers and sensitive health, mental-health, medication and sexual content. The scrubbed text is then processed by the AI providers listed below. Photos you attach are described by Google Gemini before the scrub runs on the description; voice notes are transcribed by Groq. If a note contains crisis language, Ollie stops all AI processing for it. Each provider processes the text under its own terms.

Optional anonymized data

With your explicit opt-in consent, Ollie may collect de-identified, anonymized content to improve the app and its features. Personal identifiers are removed before this data is used. This is off unless you choose to enable it, and you can change your choice at any time in the app's settings.

How we use data

  • To provide and operate the app's features.
  • To sync your content across devices, if you turn sync on (off by default; encrypted on your device first).
  • To send reminders you have enabled.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

We do not sell your personal data.

Service providers

Ollie relies on the following providers to operate, each handling data only as needed to deliver the service:

  • Clerk — sign-in and account identity.
  • Supabase — account, consent and telemetry storage; optional encrypted sync.
  • Cloudflare — app infrastructure, Ollie's AI service and its caches.
  • Voyage AI — embeddings used to sort brain dumps.
  • Groq — sorting brain dumps (main model) and voice transcription.
  • Google Gemini — fallback sorting and photo description.
  • OpenRouter — last-resort fallback sorting.
  • Anthropic — labeling of anonymized research data (only if you opt in).
  • Sentry — crash and diagnostic reporting.
  • Apple — delivery of push notifications.

Safety feature

If the app detects language suggesting you may be in crisis, it shows helpline resources only. This feature provides no medical advice and does not collect or store any related data.

Data retention

Your content is kept while your account is active. You can delete individual items in the app, or request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

Your choices

  • Manage consent for optional anonymized data in app settings.
  • Disable push notifications at any time.
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Security

We use reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Ollie is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
